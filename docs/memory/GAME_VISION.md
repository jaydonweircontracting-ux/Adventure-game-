# GAME VISION

## Identity
Large, persistent, mysterious, living open-world adventure RPG.
2D/isometric presentation. Deep simulated world.

The world continues when the player isn't watching. The player is a person
within the world, not the chosen hero. Other adventurers, workers, merchants,
travelers, residents, animals have their own activities.

Reward: curiosity, exploration, observation, interaction, preparation,
discovery, meaningful decisions.

## Inspirations (design references, not copying)
- Habbo Hotel: spatial social presence
- Endless Online: accessible persistent world feel
- Stardew Valley: daily life rhythm
- Oblivion/Skyrim: believable adventure, exploration-driven discovery
- Dwarf Fortress/WorldBox: simulated history, emergent worlds
- WoW/RuneScape: world density, readable progression
- Zelda: readable spatial exploration
- Diablo: procedural adventure, encounter design

## Restrictions
- NOT a war simulator, RTS, or kingdom-management game.
- Preserve existing architecture always.
- No constant combat arena; meaningful pace.
